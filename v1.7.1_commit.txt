fix(v1.7.1): deposit keyword corrections

Patch release. No code, API, or numerical changes — only the free-text
keywords / dcat:keyword emitted on the root dataset of future deposits.

build_keywords() in _crop_specs.R:

- PHASE deposits gain validated-uncertainty terms (uncertainty quantification,
  predictive uncertainty, prediction interval, cross-validation, calibration)
  and method terms (generalized additive model, GAM, COG, spatial data),
  matching the k-fold-calibrated uncertainty layer shipped in 1.7.0.
- Filter-variant deposits gain intermediate-product terms (station filtering,
  outlier detection, data curation) but deliberately NO uncertainty/COG terms
  they do not contain.
- Removed the inaccurate "BBCH" keyword from PHASE deposits: DWD phenological
  phase codes are crop-specific catalogue codes, not BBCH numbers.
- The hard-coded "1993-2024" temporal keyword is replaced by a range derived
  from the actual covered years (min(years)-max(years)); both builders now
  pass the real range via the new `temporal` argument to build_keywords().
