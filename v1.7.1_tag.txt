v1.7.1 — Deposit keyword corrections

Patch release. No code, API, or numerical changes; only the free-text
keywords emitted on future deposits.

- PHASE deposits gain validated-uncertainty and method keywords (uncertainty
  quantification, predictive uncertainty, prediction interval,
  cross-validation, calibration, GAM, COG, spatial data) matching the
  k-fold-calibrated uncertainty layer from 1.7.0; filter-variant deposits gain
  intermediate-product terms (station filtering, outlier detection, data
  curation) but no uncertainty/COG terms they do not contain.
- Removed the inaccurate "BBCH" keyword (DWD phase codes are crop-specific
  catalogue codes, not BBCH numbers).
- The temporal keyword is now derived from each deposit's actual year range
  rather than a hard-coded span.

Already-published 1.7.0 deposits keep their original keywords as historical
record; corrected keywords apply to future builds.
