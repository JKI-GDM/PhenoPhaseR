**v1.7.1 — deposit keyword corrections (patch)**

No code, API, or numerical changes. Only the free-text `keywords` /
`dcat:keyword` emitted on the root dataset of future deposits change.

- **PHASE deposits** gain validated-uncertainty terms (`uncertainty
  quantification`, `predictive uncertainty`, `prediction interval`,
  `cross-validation`, `calibration`) and method terms (`generalized additive
  model`, `GAM`, `COG`, `spatial data`), matching the k-fold cross-validated
  uncertainty calibration shipped in 1.7.0.
- **Filter-variant deposits** gain intermediate-product terms (`station
  filtering`, `outlier detection`, `data curation`) but deliberately carry
  **no** uncertainty/COG terms they do not contain.
- The inaccurate `BBCH` keyword is **removed**: DWD phenological phase codes
  are crop-specific catalogue codes, not BBCH numbers.
- The temporal keyword is now **derived from each deposit's actual year
  range** rather than a hard-coded `1993–2024`.

Already-published 1.7.0 deposits retain their original keyword sets as
historical record; the corrected keywords apply to future builds and to the
existing records when re-published in the coordinated ESSD release.

The PhenoPhaseR v1.7.1 source archive is on GitHub at the `v1.7.1` tag.
